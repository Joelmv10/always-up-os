## Your intro (under a minute, then stop talking)
"Thanks so much for making the time, Rich, and sorry again about last week. Quick context so you know where I'm coming from: I'm a college player here in New York, and with my dad I run Always Up. We bring youth teams from abroad to train at clubs like Atlético de Madrid and Real Sociedad, and we are starting to bring older players to train with Spanish clubs. ## Questions (in this order; skip any if time runs out)
1. "If you were in my shoes, a small brand with great partner clubs but no audience, what would you focus on first with content?"
2. "We have a real story coming: a player from Puerto Rico who came over and trained with a Spanish club for two weeks. How would you tell that story so it builds trust and doesn't sound like an ad?"
3. "Our buyers are club directors and parents, not the players. Does that change where and how you'd post? LinkedIn, Instagram, something else?"
4. "From what you saw at Arsenal and in MLS, what makes people trust a football brand they've never heard of?"
5. "What mistakes do you see small football brands make with content?"
6. "If you could only do one thing in the next 30 days, what would it be?"
