# Call with Richard Clarke (Wednesday 18/11/2026, 12:30 ET, if he confirms)

> Prepared by Claude on 17/11/2026. Rich gave a talk in one of Joel's classes. He has been Managing Editor at Arsenal, worked in MLS, Southeast Asia and the UAE, and lectures on sports business. He agreed to chat but asked two things: **don't be salesy**, and he doesn't have connections on the playing side. So this is an advice call with a mentor, not a sales call.

## Goal
Leave with 2 or 3 concrete things to do on content and brand, and a relationship good enough to send him an update in a month.

## Before the call (5 minutes)
- Have alwaysup.es and @Joel_alwaysup open, in case he wants to look.
- Notebook ready. Write down his exact words: they're useful later.
- Quiet place, camera on if it's a video call.

## Your intro (under a minute, then stop talking)
"Thanks so much for making the time, Rich. Quick context so you know where I'm coming from: I'm a college player here in New York, and with my dad I run Always Up. We bring youth teams from abroad to train at clubs like Atlético de Madrid and Real Sociedad, and we're starting to help older players get trials at Spanish clubs. The product is good and the clubs are great, but almost nobody knows who we are yet. I was away from social media for five months and I'm about to restart. That's really what I'd love your view on."

## Questions (in this order; skip any if time runs out)
1. "If you were in my shoes, a small brand with great partner clubs but no audience, what would you focus on first with content?"
2. "We have a real story coming: a player from Puerto Rico who came over and trained with a Spanish club for two weeks. How would you tell that story so it builds trust and doesn't sound like an ad?"
3. "Our buyers are club directors and parents, not the players. Does that change where and how you'd post? LinkedIn, Instagram, something else?"
4. "From what you saw at Arsenal and in MLS, what makes people trust a football brand they've never heard of?"
5. "What mistakes do you see small football brands make with content?"
6. "If you could only do one thing in the next 30 days, what would it be?"

## What not to do
- No pitch, no "would your network be interested". He told you clearly.
- Don't ask for introductions. If he offers one, say thank you and accept.
- Don't go over time: if you agreed 30 minutes, at minute 28 say "I want to respect your time".

## Closing
"This was really helpful, thank you. Would you mind if I sent you a short update in a month, once I've put some of this into practice?"
